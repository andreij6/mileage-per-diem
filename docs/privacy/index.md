# Privacy Policy: Mileage & Per Diem

**Developer:** AI.Jones · **Effective:** November 16, 2026 · **App:** Mileage & Per Diem for Android (`com.aijones.mileage`)

## Summary
Mileage & Per Diem keeps your data on your phone. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no third-party analytics. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere by itself.

## What the app stores on your device
| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise location** while a trip is recording | To draw the route and measure the distance of trips you start | Stored only on your device |
| Trip details you enter (purpose, vehicle, notes, places) | To build your trips and reports | Stored only on your device |
| Per diem stays and searches | To estimate per diem | Stored only on your device |
| Settings (name and company for reports, rates, preferences) | To personalize reports and the app | Stored only on your device |
| App diagnostics (for example startup time, crashes, feature use) | So you can see app health in Developer options | Stored only on your device, kept to the last 5,000 events, never sent anywhere |

## Location
- Location is used **only while a trip is recording**, which you always start yourself. A notification is shown the whole time, and you can stop recording from it.
- The app does **not** request background location.
- **Drive detection** (optional) uses Android's activity recognition to notice that you may be driving and shows a suggestion notification. It never records anything on its own.
- Place names on trips come from Android's built-in geocoder on your device.

## Sharing
- We do **not** sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
- Reports (PDF or CSV) and share images are created on your phone and leave it only when **you** share them through the Android share sheet, to the app you choose.

## Backups
If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include your trips and settings in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Google under your account settings, and we have no access to it.

## Your choices and deleting your data
- Turn off drive detection or change location permission at any time in the app or in Android Settings.
- Delete all trips and reports in **Settings → About & privacy → Delete all trips and reports**, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

## Children
The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children.
